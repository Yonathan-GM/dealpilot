# Technical Data Sheet - Stretch Film (SF-500-17, SF-500-23, SF-MC-20)

## Material
Linear low-density polyethylene (LLDPE), cast process, clear, cling on one side.

## Properties
| Property | SF-500-17 | SF-500-23 | SF-MC-20 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thickness | 17 micron | 23 micron | 20 micron |
| Width | 500 mm | 500 mm | 500 mm |
| Length per roll | 300 m | 300 m | 1,500 m |
| Ultimate elongation | 250% | 300% | 280% |
| Recommended pre-stretch | n/a (hand use) | n/a (hand use) | up to 250% |
| Core diameter | 76 mm | 76 mm | 76 mm |

## Storage and shelf life
Store indoors between 5 and 30 degrees Celsius, away from direct sunlight and solvents. Shelf life is 24 months from production date.

## UV exposure
This film is NOT UV-stabilised. Pallets wrapped with it should not be stored outdoors for more than 3 weeks in direct sunlight, because the film becomes brittle and loses holding force. A UV-stabilised film can be sourced on request with a 15-day lead time.

## Usage guidance
Use SF-500-23 for loads with sharp edges, such as crates or machinery. Apply at least 3 wraps at the base of the pallet and 2 wraps at the top for stable loads.
