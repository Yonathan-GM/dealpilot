# NorthPoint Industrial Supply - Pricing and Commercial Terms

## Volume discounts
Discounts apply to the total list value of a single order:
- $5,000 to $14,999: 3% discount
- $15,000 to $49,999: 6% discount
- $50,000 and above: up to 10%, negotiated by the sales manager

Orders below $5,000 receive no volume discount.

## Payment terms
- Approved accounts: Net 30 days from invoice date. Net 45 or longer is not offered as standard and needs sales manager approval.
- Early payment: a 2% discount applies if the invoice is paid within 10 days (terms written as 2/10 net 30).
- New customers: 50% advance payment with the order, balance due before dispatch.
- New accounts start with a $10,000 credit limit, reviewed after 3 months.

## Quotes and price changes
- Quotes are valid for 14 days.
- Stretch film and plastic product prices follow resin costs and are reviewed monthly. Corrugated prices are reviewed quarterly.
- Price matching is not automatic. A competitor quote must be provided in writing, and any match needs sales manager approval.

## Delivery
- Orders of $2,000 or more: free delivery within 150 km of our warehouse.
- Orders under $2,000: flat $120 delivery fee within 150 km.
- Deliveries beyond 150 km: freight is quoted separately.
- Standard delivery happens within the lead time listed for each product, counted from order confirmation.

## Returns and claims
- Unopened goods can be returned within 14 days of delivery with a 15% restocking fee.
- Custom-printed items cannot be returned.
- Damaged or incorrect goods must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We then replace the goods or issue a credit note.

## Samples
Free samples (up to 3 different items) are available to prospects with an estimated annual spend above $5,000. The customer pays for courier shipping.
