# Technical Data Sheet - Corrugated Boxes (CB-RSC-3, CB-RSC-5)

## Construction
- CB-RSC-3: single-wall, 3-ply, B-flute.
- CB-RSC-5: double-wall, 5-ply, BC-flute.

## Strength
| Property | CB-RSC-3 | CB-RSC-5 |
|---|---|---|
| Burst strength | 12 kg/cm2 | 20 kg/cm2 |
| Edge crush test (ECT) | 5.5 kN/m | 8.0 kN/m |
| Maximum load per box | 20 kg | 35 kg |
| Maximum stacking height (warehouse) | 5 boxes | 8 boxes |

## Environment
Moisture content at delivery is 8 to 10%. At sustained relative humidity near 90%, stacking strength can drop by up to 40%. For humid warehouses or sea freight, use CB-RSC-5 or add moisture-barrier coating on request (lead time +5 days).

## Printing
Custom boxes support up to 2-colour flexographic printing. A blank area for lot numbers or labels can be reserved on any panel. Minimum order for printed boxes: 3,000 units.
