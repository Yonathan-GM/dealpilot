# NorthPoint Industrial Supply - Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I place an order?** Email your purchase order to the sales desk or ask your account manager. Orders are confirmed within one business day.

**What are your support hours?** Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00. Messages sent outside those hours are answered the next business day.

**Are you certified?** Our warehouse operates under ISO 9001 quality management. Food-contact products ship with a declaration of compliance on request.

**What if a delivery is late?** Tell your account manager. For delays longer than 3 days beyond the quoted lead time, we offer a 5% credit note on the affected order.

**Can I split an order across several deliveries?** Yes. Split deliveries are allowed for orders above $5,000. Each drop is invoiced separately, and the volume discount is calculated on the total order value.

**Do you offer UV-stabilised film?** Yes, on request, with a 15-day lead time. Our standard films are not UV-stabilised.

**Do you sell to individuals?** No, we serve businesses only.
